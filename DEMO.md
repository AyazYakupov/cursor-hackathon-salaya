# Demo

Live product, no slides. Thai or English. Aim for 90 seconds of story; use the rest of the five minutes only if the first loop is already solid.

Full scope: [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

## Story in one breath

The tablet is a photo frame until the family has something to say. Today’s picture interrupts it, Betty answers with a big tap, and the people who care can see that she did.

## 90-second path (P0 only)

Props: one browser window for the caretaker, one for the patient tablet (portrait if possible). Seed a few older photos so the idle state is already pretty.

| Time | What the audience sees | What you say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Patient tablet, photos cycling | This sits in the room like a frame. Betty does not hunt for an app. |
| 0:15 | Caretaker uploads a photo, caption, priority | We went to the garden this morning. I drop the photo in, write a line, and mark it for today. |
| 0:35 | Tablet interrupts | The frame stops. She gets a check-in, not a notification drawer. |
| 0:45 | Tap → avatar, photo, spoken caption, “How are you today?” | She sees who it is from. The caption is ours. The app still asks her to check in. |
| 1:05 | Large Good / Okay / Need help tap | One tap. No typing. |
| 1:15 | Caretaker log updates | We know she answered. That photo leaves the send queue. Tomorrow we can load another, or the queue sends the next one if we do nothing. |

Stop here if anything in this path is shaky.

## If P0 is clean (P1, then P2)

**Pills.** Alert at the scheduled time. Photo of the pill (or the clump). Tap each item, then Done. Caretaker sees it logged.

**Errand.** Doctor pickup: tablet tells Betty to get ready; Bob’s panel (or a second login) pings him to leave. Say the roadmap line: other family members can each send their own check-ins later.

## Fallback if the live path breaks

Keep a short screen recording of the 90-second path. If the interrupt fails, walk the recording, then show the caretaker log on the live app if that screen still works.

Do not switch to architecture talk. If asked about more than one family sender, answer with the roadmap sentence and return to the tablet.

## Who does what in the room

- One presenter on the story.
- One teammate on the two windows: trigger the send, recover if a refresh is needed.
- Nobody else narrates.

## Seed data to prepare

- 4–6 warm photos already in history so the slideshow is not empty.
- One “today” photo with a short caption you can speak.
- One pill with a clear photo.
- One errand: “Doctor at 15:00,” responsible party Bob, patient notify 15 minutes ahead.

Rehearse twice on the machine and network you will use.
