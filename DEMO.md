# Demo

Live product, no slides. Thai or English. Aim for 90 seconds of story; use the rest of the five minutes only if the first loop is already solid.

Full scope: [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

## Story in one breath

The tablet is a photo frame until the family has something to say. Betty answers with a big tap — or she does not, and the caretaker’s phone actually buzzes.

## 90-second path (P0 only)

Props: one browser window for the caretaker, one for the patient tablet (portrait if possible), presenter’s phone with the Trello app and notifications on. Seed a few older photos so the idle state is already pretty. Both windows are already in the household. No login on stage.

| Time | What the audience sees | What you say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Patient tablet, photos cycling | This sits in the room like a frame. Betty does not hunt for an app. |
| 0:15 | Caretaker uploads a photo, caption, priority | We went to the garden this morning. I drop the photo in, write a line, and mark it for today. |
| 0:35 | Tablet interrupts | The frame stops. She gets a check-in, not a notification drawer. |
| 0:45 | Tap → avatar, photo, spoken caption, “How are you today?” | She sees who it is from. The caption is ours. The app still asks her to check in. |
| 1:05 | Large Good / Okay / Need help tap | One tap. No typing. |
| 1:15 | Caretaker log updates | We know she answered. That photo leaves the send queue. |

Then the miss beat. Do not wait a real window. Use the demo skip.

| Time | What the audience sees | What you say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:20 | New check-in, nobody taps | What if she does not pick it up? |
| 1:30 | One retry on the tablet | We nudge her again. Still nothing. |
| 1:40 | Presenter’s phone pings (Trello) | Now it comes to me. Not a mock. The phone in my hand. |

Stop here if anything in this path is shaky.

## If P0 is clean (P1, then P2)

**Pills.** Alert at the scheduled time. Photo of the pill (or the clump). Tap each item, then Done. Caretaker sees it logged.

**Errand.** Doctor pickup: tablet tells Betty to get ready; Bob’s panel pings him to leave. No second login — Bob’s window is already open if you show this. Say the roadmap line: other family members can each send their own check-ins later.

## Fallback if the live path breaks

Keep a short screen recording of the happy path. If the interrupt fails, walk the recording, then show the caretaker log on the live app if that screen still works.

If Trello does not buzz, have the board open on a third screen and move the card by hand so the audience still sees the caretaker step. Then put the phone away. Do not debug notifications on stage.

Do not switch to architecture talk. If asked about more than one family sender, answer with the roadmap sentence and return to the tablet.

## Who does what in the room

- One presenter on the story, phone face-up, ringer/notifications on.
- One teammate on the two windows: trigger the send, fire the miss-skip, recover if a refresh is needed.
- Nobody else narrates.

## Seed data to prepare

- 4–6 warm photos already in history so the slideshow is not empty.
- One “today” photo with a short caption you can speak.
- One pill with a clear photo.
- One errand: “Doctor at 15:00,” responsible party Bob, patient notify 15 minutes ahead.
- Trello board ready. One test card already pinged the presenter’s phone on this wifi.
- Demo skip control rehearsed: miss → retry → caretaker Trello ping in a few seconds.

Rehearse twice on the machine and network you will use. Confirm the phone buzzes from the back of the room.
