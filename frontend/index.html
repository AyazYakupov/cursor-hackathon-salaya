What is this?

Care Companion helps families look after an older relative who lives alone.

Left: the tablet in Margaret's home. It shows family photos and asks simple questions. Right: the app her family uses to send photos and reminders, and to get an alert if she doesn't answer.

  1. Send Margaret a check-in A family photo and "How are you feeling today?" appear on her tablet.
  2. Answer as Margaret, or don't Tap Open message, then Good. Or wait 10 seconds: she gets a reminder, then the family is alerted.
  3. Send a medication reminder Tick each item on her tablet, then confirm.
  4. Watch the family app Every answer, reminder and alert is logged on the right. Nothing leaves your browser.
